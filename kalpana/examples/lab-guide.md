# Lab planner — fictional demonstration catalog


## Make Your Own Lab — SAMPLE SCOPE

The sample planner offers horticulture/plant care (life forms), robotics/electronics (machines & materials), and financial literacy/enterprise (human services). These are illustrative category mappings for planning, not proof of official course approval. Review age appropriateness, curriculum, tools, consumables and supervision with the school.

Each shared station supports two learners. Materials are reusable across scheduled batches. A two-room plan with both class groups active provisions stations in each room. Horticulture requires a water/drainage review. Materials-only and existing-room setups exclude fit-out. The infrastructure option includes a room-level allowance, not a final construction or electrical-work offer.


## Lab planning sample prices — DEMO ONLY

One-time prices in INR, all fictional: shared station foundation 1,500 per station; horticulture materials 800 per station; robotics/electronics materials 1,800 per station; financial literacy/enterprise materials 400 per station; room fit-out allowance 30,000 per active room; optional AI extension 600 per station. Two learners share a station. These sample allowances are not market estimates or a commercial offer.
