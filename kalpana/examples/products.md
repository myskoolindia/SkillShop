# Sample learning products — DEMO ONLY

Source ID: demo-products
Revision: demo-v1
These are fictional examples for testing Kalpana. They are not RoboQuest offers.

**Digital Foundations** provides offline digital coding activities for up to 30 learners per package. It includes coding and offline access.

**Robotics Classroom** includes digital activities, robotics content and teacher training for up to 30 learners per package. It includes coding, robotics, offline access and teacher training.

**Complete Innovation Lab** includes coding, robotics, AI, offline access and teacher training for up to 60 learners per package.

Optional add-ons are teacher training and AI activities. AI activities are compatible with Robotics Classroom. A user may increase package quantity; fixed core package components cannot be removed.
